## A Beautiful Theme for Your Digital Garden

> "The best writing is rewriting." - E.B. White

Remsleep is a Hugo Lanyon-inspired theme for [Quartz v4](https://quartz.jzhao.xyz/) that brings a clean, focused reading experience to your digital garden. It combines the classic elegance of the Hugo Lanyon theme with modern features for knowledge management and exploration.

![Remsleep Theme](https://img.shields.io/badge/Quartz-v4.5.2-blue) ![License](https://img.shields.io/badge/license-MIT-green)

## Why Remsleep?

The theme prioritizes **readability** and **discoverability**:

- **Clean, focused layout** - Narrow content column (38-42rem) optimized for reading
- **Smart navigation** - Fixed right sidebar with integrated search and custom sections
- **Filterable posts** - Dynamic tag filtering without page reloads
- **Preview drawer** - Explore linked content without losing your place
- **Responsive design** - Beautiful on desktop, tablet, and mobile

## Key Features

### Fixed Right Sidebar Navigation

A sleek navigation panel styled in Hugo Lanyon's signature blue (`#234bc2`) with:
- Home button and customizable section links
- Integrated search functionality
- Posts link and dark mode toggle
- Optional GitHub link and copyright notice

### Dynamic Posts Filtering

The PostsListWithFilter component transforms your blog:
- Click tags to instantly filter posts
- Pagination for easy browsing
- Support for multiple post sources (e.g., `posts/` and `questions/`)
- Configurable curated tag list

### Preview Drawer

Click internal links to open a slide-out preview panel instead of navigating away - perfect for exploring connections in your digital garden.

### Mobile-First Design

On mobile devices, enjoy a fixed header bar with:
- Site title and dark mode toggle
- Search icon
- Hamburger menu with slide-down navigation and backdrop blur
